OUR STORY

From a garage to global infrastructure

What started as a visibility tool for a single logistics company grew into the platform powering supply chains on every continent.

2013
Founded in Singapore

Three logistics engineers quit their jobs to build real-time container tracking. First client: a 40-container shipping company.

2016
Series A & AI Labs

$18M raised. Launched our first demand forecasting engine trained on 5 billion shipment records.

2019
1,000-Client Milestone

Expanded to Europe and North America. Added warehouse robotics integration and compliance engine.

2023
Series D · $220M

Named #1 Supply Chain Platform by Gartner. Launched NexChain Control Tower — unified visibility for every node.

2026
Global Scale · 180+ Countries

2,400 enterprise clients. 420 team members across 12 offices. Processing 40M+ events per day.

MISSION & VALUES

What drives every decision we make

01

Radical Transparency

We share our model accuracy scores, uptime history and pricing openly. No hidden fees, no misleading benchmarks.

02

Customer Obsession

Every feature ships because a client requested it. Our roadmap is driven entirely by real operational pain, not trend-chasing.

Engineering Excellence
